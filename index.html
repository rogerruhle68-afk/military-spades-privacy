Military Spades — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Military Spades does not collect, track, or share any personal information.

What the app stores

Your game progress, scores, and settings (such as your chosen language) are saved only on your device, so you can pick up where you left off. This data never leaves your device.

What the app does not do

  • It has no accounts or sign-in.
  • It shows no ads and includes no analytics or tracking tools.
  • It does not use your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.
  • It does not connect to the internet or send anything to the developer or any third party.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party code or services that could collect information about you.

Contacting support

You can email support from the app's Settings screen. The message opens in your own mail app, prefilled with your app version, iOS version, and device type to help us fix problems; you can delete those details before sending. We receive only what you choose to send, and the app never sends anything on its own.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its saved data from your device.

Children

Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles data, this policy will be updated before any data is collected, and the change will be described in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@militaryspades.app
